      Range honesty audit

      The ranges on every row come from leave-one-out residuals of the price fit. A printed range is a claim; here is how often the claim held on protocols the fit had not seen.

      Every term held out twice — kept only if it passes both

      Leave-one-out (LOO) refits the model once per protocol and tests on the one left out; walk-forward (WF) fits on the oldest protocols and tests on the newest. A term is kept only when it beats the size-only baseline on LOO and on WF50. Miss is the median factor by which the fitted market cap misses the real one.

      How to read this page. The ranking is a claim about the snapshot, not about the future: the price fit misses the real market cap by a median factor of ×3.38 on protocols it has not seen, and the odds are a fitted share, not a promise. Farm verdicts are statements about one stated farmer — $2,000 of capital, a 7% drop share, gas at the high quote, five transactions, three and a half active hours over twelve weeks — not facts about the protocol. Change the ticket, the drop share or the T-bill above and the verdicts move; that is the point. Nothing here is financial advice. Data: DefiLlama snapshot as of 2026-11-14; 758 protocols at or above $5,000,000 TVL; 40 on the watchlist.

      Method, in one paragraph. The launch price is fitted from the 128 protocols whose token already trades: log market cap is quadratic in log TVL, and it is the only price term that beat the size-only baseline on leave-one-out and on walk-forward. The odds blend the share of the 10 nearest protocols by size and age that record a token (weight 0.4) with a logistic fit on size, age, 7-day change and their interaction (weight 0.6). Ranges come from leave-one-out residuals. Farm nets are capital × fitted multiple × drop share × odds, minus gas.
